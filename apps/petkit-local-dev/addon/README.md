# PetKit Local

Local cloud replacement for PetKit litter boxes, feeders, water fountains and their Bluetooth
accessories. Your devices connect to this instead of PetKit's servers: it answers as the official
API, keeps every event, photo and video on your own machine, and publishes them to Home Assistant
over MQTT discovery. It works with the internet unplugged.
